            How to play

            Goal

            Make as many real words as you can from your letters before time runs out. Words need at least 2 letters, and each tile can be used once per word.

            Scoring

            Each word scores 2 points per letter, plus a bonus for rare letters (like Z, Q, X).

            • +15 for your longest word
            • +15 for your rarest word
            • In a room, +15 to whoever found the longest word overall

            Playing together

            One person taps Create a room and shares the invite link (or the 5-letter code). Everyone else opens the link on their own phone, tablet or computer. Up to 8 players. Nobody needs an account.

            The host picks the settings and starts the game. Everyone gets the same letters and starts at the same moment. Scores update live. Words are revealed at the end.

            Keep the game open on the host's device while you play. The room closes when the host leaves.

            Languages

            English and Italian. Accents don't matter: type perche for perché. Italian includes verb forms like dimmelo and mangiarlo.
