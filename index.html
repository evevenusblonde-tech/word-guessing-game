    How to Play

    Goal

    Find as many valid words as you can before your timer ends. Words can be as short as 2 letters.

    Letters

    Choose 3 to 10 letters at the start. Every word must use only the available letters, and each tile can only be used as many times as it appears.

    Scoring

    Each accepted word scores from its length and letter rarity: 2 points per letter, plus a rarity bonus based on the letters used.

    • +15 for your longest word at the end of the run.
    • +15 for your most unusual word at the end of the run.
    • In online rooms, +15 goes to the player or players with the longest word among everyone.

    Online Rooms

    The host opens the room, then each player taps Start My Timer when ready. Scores are finalized only after every active player has finished.

    Leaderboards

    Leaderboards are permanent, limited to the top 10, and separated by run length and language.
